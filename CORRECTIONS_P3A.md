# Corrections (Paper 3A)

Every defect in Paper 3A's design, code or text found after it was written, why it was
wrong, and what fixed it. Entries are never deleted. Numbering starts at P3A-1.

| # | Defect | Fix | How it was caught |
|---|---|---|---|
| **P3A-1** | *Our own error: the Henry-limit test checked the limit at a pressure that is not the limit.* The first harness tested q*/p against the model's K(T) at ln p = -60. For random parameters, a cooperative term with an exponent barely above 1 still contributed there, so the test failed although the limit is exact. The design also allowed cooperative exponents arbitrarily close to 1, so a "cooperative" term could imitate a Henry term at every accessible pressure. | Cooperative exponents are bounded below, g >= 1.5 (`models.PotentialIsotherm.g_min`). The test evaluates the limit at ln p = -300. `docs/DESIGN.md` now states that the exact limit does not mean the data reach the Henry regime. Caught before any real data were used. | *Found 2026-10-12 by the property test over 20 random parameter draws (seed 0 failed).* |
| **P3A-2** | *A design weakness: the first proposed model let a free network carry the temperature dependence.* Convexity and the Henry limit do not constrain how the isotherm moves with temperature. On a synthetic two-site isotherm the free network fitted the training temperatures to an RMSE below 10^-3 mol/kg, yet its step position bent with temperature and the maximum error was above 1 mol/kg at 10 K outside the training range, on both sides (`scripts/diag_tmode_extrapolation.py`, `logs/diag_tmode_extrapolation.log`). | The model's temperature dependence is now thermodynamic: an integrated van't Hoff law per term, optionally with a constant heat capacity (`t_mode="vanthoff"`, `"kirchhoff"`). The free network stays as `t_mode="nn"`, and the study will cross the shape constraint (none, penalty, hard) with the temperature structure (free, van't Hoff, characteristic curve) so that each ingredient's contribution is measured separately. Synthetic only; nothing confirmatory had run. | *Found 2026-10-13 when the synthetic recovery test failed and the diagnostic separated a fitting failure from an extrapolation failure.* |
| **P3A-3** | *Our own error: seeds that did not vary anything.* In `t_mode="vanthoff"` and `"kirchhoff"` the parameters were initialised to constants, so three "seeds" gave identical fits (`logs/diag_tmode_extrapolation.log`, the vanthoff and kirchhoff rows). A seed count would have claimed a robustness check that never happened. | The capacities, positions and heats now start from a seeded random jitter (`models.PotentialIsotherm.__init__`). The pre-registration will require every reported seed spread to come from runs whose initial parameters differ, checked in code. | *Found 2026-10-13 by reading the diagnostic log: identical digits across seeds.* |
| **P3A-4** | *Our own error: a wrong DOI in the lead's brief.* The data-inventory brief gave COF-432 as 10.1021/jacs.0c07062, written from memory. Crossref returns 404 for it. The paper is Nguyen et al., J. Am. Chem. Soc. 2020, 142, 2218, DOI 10.1021/jacs.9b13094. | The inventory uses the Crossref-verified DOI. Every DOI entering the manuscript must pass the citation gate (Crossref title match), never memory. | *Found 2026-10-13 by the inventory script's Crossref check, confirmed by the lead.* |
| **P3A-5** | *Our own error: a personal email address sent to third-party services.* The lead's agent briefs told the agents to identify themselves to Unpaywall and OpenAlex with the author's university address, and one OpenAlex call by the lead did the same. This broke the author's standing rule. | The address is removed from `data/scripts/isodb_water_inventory.py` and `data/scripts/primary_doi_check.py`. Unpaywall now gets a neutral placeholder and OpenAlex gets no address. Reported to the author at once. | *Found 2026-10-13 by the lead on reading the inventory agent's report.* |
| **P3A-6** | *Our own errors: the vector digitiser calibrated axes on the wrong labels, twice.* (i) `numeric_labels` took every text span touching the label strip, so the y axis's "0" at the plot corner was used as an x tick. On Mg-SU-102 Fig. 4a this gave an x-calibration residual of 4 pt and pressures off by up to 0.5 mbar, while recall looked plausible (0.88). (ii) Where a figure has no tick marks, label box centres were used; a centred label's box depends on glyph widths, so "10" and "12" sat about 1 pt off the single-digit spacing (Co2Cl2(BTDD) SI Fig. S8, residual 0.25 pt). | (i) A label counts only if its centre lies inside the strip (regression test `test_a_corner_label_of_the_other_axis_is_not_taken`). After the fix the vector extraction reproduces the Mg-SU-102 numeric source data: 198 of 198 points, RMS 1e-5 g/g and 0.005 mbar. (ii) Without tick marks, only labels of the modal text length calibrate the axis (residual 0.04 pt). No model had seen any digitised data. | *Found 2026-11-03 by comparing the vector extraction with numeric source data, then by reading each figure's calibration residual.* |
| **P3A-7** | *Our own errors: the first raster detector found desorption markers as adsorption points, and its first benchmark mismeasured recall.* (i) Colour blobs after a morphological opening deleted hollow markers and merged crowded markers. On Mg-SU-102 rendered at 150 to 600 dpi it recovered about half the points with errors of 3 to 6 % of the axis. (ii) The disc-template detector that replaced it reported false detections in dense figures, about 2.8 % of the axis off the curve, which is the offset of the desorption branch. A bias there moves the apparent step to lower pressure, which is the quantity that changes with temperature. (iii) The first synthetic benchmark gave three temperatures identical plateaus, so markers were completely hidden and recall measured occlusion, not the detector. | (i, ii) Template matching with the figure's own marker footprint (cut from an isolated marker, usually the legend), a white-core test, and a veto of filled detections centred on an open marker of the same colour (`digitise.raster_markers`). No physical prior (such as monotonicity) is used to filter points, because that could leak into the tests. (iii) Realistic plateau separations; recall reported over all points and over points at least 30 % visible. Remaining worst case (800 px, about 80 points per isotherm, desorption on top): 2 to 3 % false points, each within about 2.8 % of the axis; reported as such in the error model. | *Found 2026-11-03 by benchmark runs and by inspecting a crop around a false detection.* |
| **P3A-8** | *A coverage rule that took three tries, all before any fit.* The digitiser recovers only visible adsorption markers. In Hanikel 2019 SI Fig. S17 the open desorption circles cover the 10, 25 and 35 C plateaus, leaving those isotherms with only their steps. A rule was needed. (i) The first version also demanded no gap in uptake above 0.25 q_ref and a first point below 0.25 q_ref. That excluded real measurements: Co2Cl2(BTDD), KMF-1 and Mg-SU-102 have genuinely steep steps, and Mg-SU-102's lowest pressure is 20 Pa. (ii) The second version required the first point at p/psat <= 0.10. That excluded COF-432 at 25 C, whose adsorption series starts at 0.21 with 1 % of q_ref; the vector data show that nothing is hidden there. (iii) The gap test ran after the 0.90 cut, so MOF-303 at 10 C, whose last visible point is at 0.971, passed with its plateau hidden. | Final rule 5 (PREREG section 3.3): on the curve as published, no gap above 0.25 in p/psat, and a start at p/psat <= 0.10 or at uptake <= 0.10 q_ref. It flags all three occluded MOF-303 isotherms and passes the real steep steps, flat pre-step regions and pressure-limited isotherms. Each version was judged only on whether it separated occlusion from physics in the digitised data; no model had been fitted to any of it. | *Found 2026-11-03 by reading the builder's per-temperature reasons against the overlays and vector data.* |
