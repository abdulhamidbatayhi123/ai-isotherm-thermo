## Why each fits

1. **Jirasek**: HANNA builds thermodynamic consistency into the network architecture instead of adding a penalty, which is the hard-versus-soft choice this paper tests.
2. **Shimizu**: develops statistical-thermodynamic theory of sorption isotherms and their temperature dependence. He can judge the Henry limit, the adsorbed-phase potential and the isosteric-heat claims. He is cited (Shimizu and Matubayasi 2021), but a different paper is listed here.
3. **Janiak**: measures water sorption in MOFs for heat transformation and co-wrote the isosteric-heat guide the paper relies on. He can judge the measured isotherms and the Qst analysis.
4. **Snurr**: an authority on adsorption in MOFs with machine learning and simulation of water uptake. He can judge the benchmark design and the realism of the synthetic truths. Note: his JPCC 2026 co-author list includes O. K. Farha, and his 2024 papers include S. Keskin (Koç University, Türkiye), who is not a collaborator of the author.
5. **Wang**: leads applied work on sorption heat pumps and MOF water harvesting. He can say whether the extrapolation errors matter for cycle and device design across temperatures.
6. **Kim**: leads machine learning for MOFs (reviews, transferable models). He can judge reproducibility, baselines and claims of novelty against the MOF-ML literature.
- **A1 Mitsos**: senior author of the Gibbs-Duhem-informed and thermodynamics-consistent networks the manuscript cites (Rittig and Mitsos). He works in a different group from Jirasek.
- **A2 Dincă**: designs MOF water sorbents and works on cycle-level water-uptake behaviour. He is the Americas alternate for (c).

Spread: Europe has 3 (DE, UK, DE), the Americas 1 and Asia 2 (CN, KR). Both alternates are in Europe or the USA. No two come from the same group. Only Jirasek and Dincă (A2) have a listed paper that the manuscript cites. Mitsos is a cited author through other papers.

Caveats: the RPTU page gives Jirasek's title as "Director" with "Prof. Dr.-Ing.". An older RPTU page still showing "Junior Professor" is outdated. Janiak's position comes from his CV PDF on the group site, which has a 2026 file date. I found no current institutional profile for Florence Vermeire (KU Leuven), so she is not listed.
